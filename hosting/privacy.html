Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · PromptIndo (“the app”)

What we collect

  • Account data: email address (kept in a private Firestore collection), display name, username, biography and optional profile photo.
  • Content you create: prompts, comments, likes, bookmarks and follows.
  • Device data: Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens for notifications you enable, and App Check / Play Integrity signals used solely to prevent abuse.
  • Usage data: anonymous view identifiers (randomly generated, not tied to your identity) used for trending calculations, deduplicated over 24 hours.

How we use it

To operate the service: showing your public profile and prompts to others, sending notifications you have opted into, moderating reported content, and understanding which prompts are trending. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Your email address is never public — it is stored separately and only readable by you (and administrators handling abuse reports). Your name, username, bio, avatar and prompts are public. Likes, bookmarks and following lists are visible to signed-in users as described in the app.

Storage & retention

Data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Storage, Authentication). You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account; this permanently removes your profile, prompts, comments and media after the deletion function completes.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
